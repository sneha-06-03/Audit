# 🔐 Data Privacy Audit: Nykaa

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents a **Data Privacy Audit of Nykaa**, an Indian e-commerce company operating in the beauty, personal care, fashion, and lifestyle sectors.

The objective of this audit is to identify potential **data privacy vulnerabilities, risks, and compliance considerations** associated with the collection, processing, storage, and sharing of customer data.

> **Note:** This is an academic/student audit. The risks identified below are areas for investigation and should not be interpreted as confirmed security incidents or legal violations.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this audit are:

- Identify the types of personal data collected by Nykaa.
- Understand how customer data may be processed and used.
- Identify potential privacy and security risks.
- Examine third-party data-sharing considerations.
- Review relevant data-protection requirements in India.
- Assess potential privacy risks using a risk matrix.
- Recommend measures to improve data privacy and security.

---

## 🏢 Organization Selected

**Company:** Nykaa

**Industry:** E-commerce / Beauty / Fashion

| Risk Area | Potential Vulnerability | Possible Impact | Priority |
|-----------|-------------------------|-----------------|----------|
| Account Security | Weak or compromised credentials | Unauthorized account access | 🔴 High |
| Customer Data | Unauthorized access to personal information | Privacy loss | 🔴 High |
| Payment Data | Exposure through security incidents or third parties | Financial fraud | 🔴 High |

| Third Parties | Data shared with external vendors | Reduced control over data | 🔴 High |

| Data Retention | Data stored longer than necessary | Increased breach impact | 🟠 Medium |
| Marketing | Unwanted promotional communication | User inconvenience | 🟡 Low–Medium |
| Mobile App | Excessive permissions or insecure storage | Unauthorized data access | 🟠 Medium |

> **Important:** These are potential risks identified for audit purposes and are not claims that Nykaa has experienced these incidents.

## 🔍 Areas Audited

### 1. Transparency

The audit considers whether users are clearly informed about:

- What data is collected
- Why the data is collected
- How the data is used
- How long data is retained
- Who the data may be shared with
- How users can exercise their privacy rights

### 2. Consent

The audit examines whether users have appropriate choices regarding:

- Marketing emails
- SMS notifications
- Cookies
- Advertising
- Personalized recommendations
- Optional data collection

### 3. Data Security

Security controls considered include:

- Encryption
- Password protection
- Access controls
- Authentication
- Security monitoring
- Vulnerability testing
- Incident response

### 4. Third-Party Data Sharing

Potential third parties may include:

- Payment service providers
- Delivery/logistics partners
- Analytics providers
- Advertising platforms
- Technology/service providers

The audit considers what information may be shared and whether appropriate safeguards are in place.

## 🇮🇳 Indian Data Protection Considerations

The audit considers India's data-protection framework, including the:

**Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)**

Relevant areas include:

- Transparency and notice
- Consent and lawful processing
- Data security
- User rights
- Children's personal data
- Personal-data breaches
- Data retention and deletion
- Responsibilities of organizations processing personal data

> Privacy regulations and implementation requirements can change. The applicable legal requirements should be verified for the date of the audit.

## 🛡️ Recommendations

The following measures could strengthen data privacy:

1. 🔑 Encourage strong passwords and multi-factor authentication.
2. 🔒 Encrypt sensitive information during transmission and storage where appropriate.
3. 📉 Minimize the amount of personal data collected.
4. 📄 Provide clear and understandable privacy notices.
5. ⚙️ Give users greater control over optional tracking and marketing.
6. 🤝 Regularly review third-party vendors and their security practices.
7. 🗑️ Establish appropriate data-retention and deletion policies.
8. 🔍 Conduct regular privacy and security assessments.
9. 🚨 Maintain a strong data-breach detection and response process.
10. 👤 Provide accessible mechanisms for users to exercise applicable privacy rights.
